# Thai Translator

A lightweight, mobile-friendly Thai ↔ English conversation interpreter.

## Use it

Open the deployed GitHub Pages site on your iPhone in Safari. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** to keep it on your home screen.

- Choose **Thai speaks** or **English speaks**.
- Tap the microphone and speak one sentence at a time, or type a sentence.
- The app shows the original and translation together and reads the translation aloud.
- Use **Replay translation** to hear a translation again.

## Notes

- An internet connection is required for translation.
- Microphone input requires browser support and microphone permission. Safari support can vary by iOS version.
- Speech playback uses voices available on the device.
- This is a convenience tool, not a certified interpreter. Use a qualified interpreter for medical, legal, or other high-stakes conversations.
